Our products

What we build and maintain at GD3V.

eSchuul

A platform where schools and training centers set up their own virtual school, publish courses, and manage their students. Students enroll, follow their classes, and receive class alerts through the web portal and the mobile application.

KENFACK

An assistant that lets eSchuul users get help and manage their account through WhatsApp. It only replies to people who contact it first.

How we use WhatsApp

Users always start the conversation themselves. To connect, a user taps "Connect WhatsApp" in the eSchuul portal, which opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message to our number. KENFACK only replies to people who contact it. We do not send promotional or bulk messages.

Users can disconnect WhatsApp from their eSchuul account at any time. See our Privacy Policy for details on the data we handle and how to request deletion.
